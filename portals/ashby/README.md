# Ashby

The job boards of companies you choose that hire with Ashby. Dossier reads each board's list of open jobs, with their whole text and the pay where the company shows it, and matches it to your search on your server: the search itself isn't sent anywhere.

- **Terms:** https://developers.ashbyhq.com/docs/public-job-posting-api (public, without authentication, for the job postings a company publishes)
- **Countries:** Every country
- **Kinds of work:** any
- **Access:** Official API
- **Key:** None. Add the companies' boards in Settings → Job search, one a line: a board's name, as in `jobs.ashbyhq.com/ramp`, or a link to it. Up to 25.
- **Credit:** None asked for. Each posting links to its page on the company's board.

Dossier asks up to four boards at a time and keeps each board's answer for half an hour. A board that can't be read is skipped and named.
