# Remotive

Remote jobs from Remotive's public API, published a day after they're posted on Remotive. Dossier reads the list whole and matches it to your search on your server: every word in the title or company, Remote only, the country the job can be done from, and how recent.

- **Terms:** https://remotive.com/api-documentation (link back to the job on Remotive and mention Remotive as the source; don't pass the jobs on to other job sites; at most four requests a day)
- **Countries:** Every country
- **Kinds of work:** any
- **Access:** Official API
- **Key:** None
- **Credit:** "Jobs from Remotive", linked to remotive.com. Each posting links to its page on Remotive.

Dossier downloads the list at most every six hours, shared by every search, so four times a day at most.
